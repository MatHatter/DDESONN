DDESONN 7.3.10: Select completed runs and separate attempt status

A failed best-checkpoint attempt could leave an error next to another run's usable final-epoch model and chart. The workbench now gives the latest attempt its own numbered status and error area, while completed runs are selectable with radio buttons in the existing completed-model panel below Training progress. Build & Train stays on the left and the chart stays at the upper right.

Selecting a completed run restores its training controls and activates its own loss history, checkpoint summary, evaluation, prediction, warm-start source, model download and saved recipe. The selected chart remains visible during another attempt; a legacy bundle without history shows an explicit empty state. Edits configure the next new run on the currently prepared data. Loaded bundles and successful warm starts add completed entries; failed and cancelled attempts preserve every earlier model. Starting a new attempt clears the active warning, and attempt history retains older outcomes and expandable technical details. History is isolated per session and held in memory; downloading a bundle saves the selected model.

Hover uses a normal cursor, an enlarged epoch dot and a loss tooltip. It neither redraws the plot nor selects/restores weights. Missing-validation-checkpoint errors now have specific guidance, with no automatic fallback to final weights. This change does not diagnose the cause of an earlier user's failed model from the screenshot alone or alter the training mathematics.
